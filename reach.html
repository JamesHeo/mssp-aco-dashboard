ACO Type Definitions

🏥 High Needs Population ACO

ACOs specializing in beneficiaries with complex medical needs, including multiple chronic conditions and dual-eligible (Medicare + Medicaid) status. Uses an integrated care model similar to PACE. Lower minimum beneficiary threshold (PY2026: 1,400).

👥 Standard ACO

Designed for experienced organizations serving traditional Medicare FFS population. Includes participants with prior MSSP, NGACO, or Pioneer ACO experience. Minimum 5,000 aligned beneficiaries required.

✨ New Entrant ACO

For organizations with limited Medicare FFS experience or those entering risk-based models for the first time. More than 50% of providers cannot have prior MSSP/NGACO experience. Must progressively increase beneficiary counts.
